* How to cite rgpycrumbs

If you use rgpycrumbs in your research, please cite the relevant papers based on functionality used.

** Software citation

cite:[goswami2024rgpycrumbs]

** Visualization

When using 2D reaction valley projection (landscape visualization, =grad_imq=, =grad_matern=, Nystrom approximation):

cite:[goswami2026valley]

The method maps nudged elastic band (NEB) trajectories onto two coordinates: the permutation-corrected root mean square deviation (RMSD) from the reactant and from the product.
A rotated frame turns those into the reaction progress (=s=) and the orthogonal deviation (=d=).

** Nudged elastic band

When using NEB orchestration or enhanced CI-NEB:

cite:[goswami2026neb]

Enhanced Climbing Image NEB method with Hessian eigenmode alignment.

** Gaussian processes

When using surface fitting, GP models, or saddle search acceleration:

cite:[goswami2025gpr goswami2025pruning]

- GPR-accelerated saddle point searches (Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation, 2025)
- Adaptive pruning for robustness (ChemPhysChem 2025)
