#+TITLE: Thermal orbit stationarity
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil todo:nil num:nil author:nil

The ~eon orbit-residual~ command evaluates the Euclidean equation of motion
on the orbit written by an instanton solver. The residual in each mobile
Cartesian coordinate is the physical force plus the mass times the second
imaginary-time derivative of the position. Output includes both the largest
absolute component and the root mean square across components and beads,
in eV/Å.

* Inputs

Supply a readcon band containing a complete thermal period, with one
~tau_over_beta~ fraction in each frame. Place the beads at uniform intervals
and omit a repeated endpoint. Use continuous atomic coordinates across the
period. The command reads masses, atom IDs, fixed masks and cell geometry
from the band. If frames do not specify ~temperature_K~, supply
~--temperature~ in kelvin.

The independent force provider is a factory named by ~module:function~ or
~/path/to/file.py:function~. It receives the band path and keyword arguments
from ~--options~. Its ~cartesian_forces(frame)~ method returns an array of
shape ~(atoms, 3)~ in eV/Å. ~result_counts()~ is optional and returns
cumulative ~computed~ and ~served~ geometry counts. The report records the
work of the independent check under ~evaluation_counts.scope = validation~.

#+begin_src bash
rgpycrumbs eon orbit-residual ring.con \
    --provider potential.py:orbit_provider --temperature 150
#+end_src

A stationary saddle repeated at every bead can have a small force residual.
The ~geometry_extent~ record gives the number of distinct coordinate arrays,
the largest single-atom displacement between any two beads in Å, and the
largest displacement in mass-weighted Cartesian space in Å √u. All three use
the coordinates as stored. Independent rotations or translations of beads
contribute to the measured span. To track an internal coordinate, add
~--atom-pair 2 5~ with the two atom IDs from the band. The ~pair_distances~
record reports their minimum and maximum separation in Å over the period.

The calculation differentiates the periodic Fourier interpolant on the bead
grid. Repeating the check at higher bead resolution distinguishes a
discretization residual from an error that persists in the path.
