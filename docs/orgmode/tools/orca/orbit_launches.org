#+title: Physical ORCA launches along an instanton orbit

A native eOn run with persistent caching records every requested ring
gradient in its readcon orbit and every ORCA subprocess in its launch
directory. To determine how many of those requests reached ORCA, provide
the optimized image band, the full orbit, the native job ledger and a tar
archive of the launches. Each archive directory must preserve its original
input timestamp and contain =input.inp= and =launch.json=.

#+begin_src shell
rgpycrumbs orca orbit-launches lineb.con lineb-orbit.con native.json \
  engrad-launches.tgz > orbit-launches.json
#+end_src

The audit checks species order, one normally terminated SCF and EnGrad per
launch, and the complete central Cartesian displacement stencil for the
image Hessians. The ORCA input coordinates identify each launch within
their printed decimal precision. The full-precision readcon coordinates
identify repeated orbit requests. Each physical launch is assigned to the
path, Hessian, central ring or output part of the job.

For a rate job seeded from a path-only cache, pass the path-only ORCA
archive with =--inherited-launches=. Its input decks can account for a
ring geometry served from the inherited cache. The report records its
checksum and counts the rate job's physical launches separately.

The separate reflection estimate holds every outward bead and both turns
fixed and uses the corresponding outward coordinates exactly on the
return leg. The changed launch count is a prediction about a cold run
under that assumption. Analysis of the archived data makes no additional
surface requests.
