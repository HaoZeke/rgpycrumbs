#+TITLE: Saddle search visualization
#+AUTHOR: Rohit Goswami
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil todo:nil num:nil author:nil

This tutorial plots one eOn saddle search with ~rgpycrumbs eon plt-saddle~.
The system is ethane, and the search climbs from the staggered minimum to the eclipsed saddle point of the rotation about the C-C bond.
Each figure comes from the command printed above it.

The two-dimensional map in this tutorial follows R. Goswami, "Two-dimensional RMSD projections for reaction path visualization and validation", /MethodsX/ (2026), [[https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mex.2026.103851][doi:10.1016/j.mex.2026.103851]].
Please cite that paper if you use these maps in published figures.

* Data and environment

The eOn output files for this tutorial are in =docs/orgmode/tutorials/data/ethane/saddle_search/=.
The script =docs/generate_singleended_data.py= computes them with eOn 2.16 and its default dimer settings.
The energies and forces come from the GFN2-xTB tight-binding method through the =ext_pot= interface of eOn.

| File | Content |
|------+---------|
| =pos.con= | Staggered minimum, the reactant |
| =displacement.con= | Minimum moved 0.2 Å along the C-C torsion mode, where the search starts |
| =direction.dat= | Starting direction of the dimer, the same torsion mode |
| =climb.con= | One frame per iteration, from frame 0 at the start to frame 38 at the saddle point |
| =saddle.con= | Saddle point |
| =mode.dat= | Dimer direction at the saddle point |
| =results.dat= | Summary of the run |
| =config.ini= | eOn settings |

The header line of each frame in =climb.con= records the energy above the reactant, the lowest curvature, the step size and the convergence value of that iteration.
~plt-saddle~ reads the plotted values from these lines.

The landscape also needs the structure in each frame, which ~plt-saddle~ compares with the reactant and the saddle point through Iterative Rotations and Assignments (IRA).
IRA aligns two structures and matches their atoms.
The repository's pixi =fragments= environment provides IRA, and the figures on this page come from that environment.
Run the commands from the tutorial folder.

#+begin_src bash
git clone https://github.com/HaoZeke/rgpycrumbs
cd rgpycrumbs
pixi shell -e fragments
cd docs/orgmode/tutorials
#+end_src

Outside pixi, install IRA from conda-forge, where the package is named =ira=.
Without IRA, ~plt-saddle~ still draws the profile and the convergence panels, but the landscape command stops with an error.

* Energy and curvature profile

#+begin_src bash
rgpycrumbs eon plt-saddle --job-dir data/ethane/saddle_search --plot-type profile \
  -o saddle_profile.png
#+end_src

#+begin_export rst
.. image:: /_static/tutorial/saddle_profile.png
   :alt: Two panels against iteration. The energy rises to 1.3 eV at iteration 20 and settles near 0.11 eV, and the lowest curvature stays positive until iteration 27 and then turns negative.
#+end_export

The left panel shows the energy above the reactant at each iteration.
The right panel shows the lowest curvature that the dimer finds there, in eV/Å².
This curvature is positive until iteration 27, which means that the energy curves upward in every direction, as it does near a minimum.
In such a region the dimer method can only climb along its direction, and every step has the largest size that eOn allows, 0.2 Å.

The torsion closes from 50.8 to 5.6 degrees within the first five steps.
After that the torsion stays within 20 degrees of the eclipsed form, but the bonds stretch.
At iteration 20 the C-C bond is 1.623 Å long and the six C-H bonds are 1.194 Å long, against 1.521 Å and 1.088 Å in the minimum, and the energy reaches 1.306 eV.
From iteration 27 the curvature is negative, the steps shrink below 0.03 Å, and the search converges on the eclipsed saddle point at iteration 38.
The saddle point lies 0.112 eV above the minimum, and its curvature along the dimer direction is -0.310 eV/Å².

* Landscape

#+begin_src bash
rgpycrumbs eon plt-saddle --job-dir data/ethane/saddle_search --plot-type landscape \
  --surface-type grad_matern --project-path --plot-structures endpoints \
  -o saddle_landscape.png
#+end_src

#+begin_export rst
.. image:: /_static/tutorial/saddle_landscape.png
   :alt: The search path on progress and deviation coordinates, from R at the left to SP at the right, over a speckled background, with ball-and-stick structures of the reactant and the saddle point below.
#+end_export

The landscape places each frame by its root mean square deviation (RMSD) from the reactant and from the saddle point.
The =--project-path= flag turns these two distances into progress /s/ along the line from the first frame to the saddle point and deviation /d/ from that line.
The =--plot-structures endpoints= option draws the reactant and the saddle point under the map.

The first five steps stay within 0.013 Å of /d/ = 0 as the torsion closes.
Frames 6 to 25 then wander near the saddle point, up to 0.095 Å off the line, and the last 13 frames differ from the saddle point by an RMSD of at most 0.020 Å.
The map hides part of this wandering.
Frames 7 and 17 lie 0.047 Å apart on it, yet frame 17 sits 0.835 eV higher, with C-H bonds of 1.175 Å against 1.104 Å in frame 7.

The background is a fitted surface, by default a Gaussian process (GP) with a gradient-enhanced Matérn kernel.
The GP takes the energy of each frame, and a slope from the energy change between the neighbouring frames along the direction of the path in the map.
For this search the fit fails.
Its length scale is about 2000 Å, far beyond the 0.4 Å that the frames span.
The fitted surface is flat at 0.319 eV, the mean energy of the frames.
~plt-saddle~ then stretches the whole color scale over the 0.00004 eV by which this flat surface varies across the map.
The speckle therefore carries no energy information.
The dashed variance contours do not help either, because the variance that they trace changes by only 2% across the map.
On this map, read the energies from the colors of the path, which follow the colorbar.

* Convergence panel

#+begin_src bash
rgpycrumbs eon plt-saddle --job-dir data/ethane/saddle_search --plot-type convergence \
  -o saddle_convergence.png
#+end_src

#+begin_export rst
.. image:: /_static/tutorial/saddle_convergence.png
   :alt: Two panels against iteration. The convergence value on a log scale rises to about 7 by iteration 20 and falls to 0.002 at iteration 38, and the step size stays at 0.2 until iteration 26 and then drops close to zero.
#+end_export

The left panel shows the convergence value, which eOn compares with its =converged_force= threshold of 0.01 eV/Å.
The right panel shows the step size.
The convergence value peaks at 6.84 eV/Å at iteration 20 and drops below the threshold only at iteration 38, at 0.0021 eV/Å.
The steps of 0.2 Å up to iteration 26 match the iterations with positive curvature in the profile.

* Option summary

| Option | Effect |
|--------+--------|
| =--plot-type profile= | Energy and lowest curvature against iteration |
| =--plot-type landscape= | Path on the RMSD map over a fitted surface |
| =--plot-type convergence= | Convergence value and step size against iteration |
| =--surface-type= | Surface fit: =grad_matern= by default, or =grad_imq= or =rbf= |
| =--ref-product= | Second reference structure in place of the saddle point |
| =--no-project-path= | The two RMSD values instead of progress and deviation |
| =--plot-structures endpoints= | Reactant and saddle point under the landscape |
| =--job-dir= with =--label= | Another search on the same plots |
