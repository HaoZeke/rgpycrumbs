These three figures show options of =rgpycrumbs eon plt-neb= that the [[file:neb-visualization.org][nudged elastic band tutorial]] leaves out.
They draw the same Diels-Alder band, from the eOn output in =docs/orgmode/tutorials/data/diels_alder/=, and the commands run from =docs/orgmode/tutorials/=.
=docs/generate_figure_gallery.py= runs the same commands to make the images on this page.

* Energies in kJ/mol

=--energy-unit kJ/mol= gives the energies in kJ/mol, and =--xyzrender-config tube= draws the structures with the =tube= preset of ~xyzrender~, as bonds without atoms.

#+begin_src bash
rgpycrumbs eon plt-neb --plot-type profile --energy-unit kJ/mol \
  --plot-structures crit_points --xyzrender-config tube \
  --input-dat-pattern "data/diels_alder/neb_*.dat" \
  --input-path-pattern "data/diels_alder/neb_path_*.con" \
  --con-file data/diels_alder/neb.con --sp-file data/diels_alder/sp.con \
  -o profile_kj_tube.png
#+end_src

#+begin_export rst
.. image:: /_static/gallery/profile_kj_tube.png
   :alt: Energy profiles of four snapshots of the band in kJ/mol, with tube drawings of the reactant, the saddle point and the product below.
#+end_export

The barrier of the final band reads 76 kJ/mol, which is the 0.788 eV of the tutorial.

* Structures under the path map

With =--plot-structures crit_points=, the path map gets the same strip as the profile: the reactant, the saddle point and the product, drawn with the default preset of ~xyzrender~.

#+begin_src bash
rgpycrumbs eon plt-neb --plot-type landscape --landscape-mode path --project-path \
  --plot-structures crit_points \
  --input-dat-pattern "data/diels_alder/neb_*.dat" \
  --input-path-pattern "data/diels_alder/neb_path_*.con" \
  --con-file data/diels_alder/neb.con --sp-file data/diels_alder/sp.con \
  -o landscape_path_strip.png
#+end_src

#+begin_export rst
.. image:: /_static/gallery/landscape_path_strip.png
   :alt: The final band on the progress and deviation axes, colored by energy, with ball-and-stick drawings of the reactant, the saddle point and the product below.
#+end_export

* Inverse multiquadric surface

=--surface-type grad_imq= fits a Gaussian process (GP) with a gradient-enhanced inverse multiquadric (IMQ) kernel, where the tutorial fits one with a Matérn kernel.

#+begin_src bash
rgpycrumbs eon plt-neb --plot-type landscape --landscape-mode surface \
  --surface-type grad_imq --project-path \
  --input-dat-pattern "data/diels_alder/neb_*.dat" \
  --input-path-pattern "data/diels_alder/neb_path_*.con" \
  --con-file data/diels_alder/neb.con --sp-file data/diels_alder/sp.con \
  -o surface_grad_imq.png
#+end_src

#+begin_export rst
.. image:: /_static/gallery/surface_grad_imq.png
   :alt: A fitted energy surface on the progress and deviation axes, with dashed variance contours and the final band on top.
#+end_export

The IMQ surface spans -2.16 eV to 0.19 eV over the map.
It reaches down to the product valley, but its highest point lies 0.60 eV below the saddle point, at 0.79 eV.
As in the tutorial, the surface colors span the range of the surface.
The colorbar covers the energies of the band, from -1.95 eV to 0.79 eV.
